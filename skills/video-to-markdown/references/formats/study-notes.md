# Format: study notes

For lectures, talks, and explainers. The reader wants the ideas fast, with a path back to the video for detail.

Template: [assets/templates/study-notes.md](../../assets/templates/study-notes.md)

## Structure

1. **Overview:** two or three sentences on what the video covers and who it is for.
2. **Key points:** five to ten bullets, each one a complete takeaway.
3. **Sections:** one per topic, each with a timestamp link, a short explanation, and the frames that matter.
4. **Code and commands:** every snippet shown, in fenced blocks.
5. **Terms:** a short glossary when the video introduces vocabulary.
6. **Resources:** links the speaker mentions.
7. **Open questions:** things the video leaves unanswered.

## Rules

- Write in your own words; short quotes are fine when the wording matters.
- Lead each section with its conclusion, then the support.
- Use bullets for lists of three or more items; use prose for reasoning.
- Mark opinions as the speaker's ("The speaker argues ...").
