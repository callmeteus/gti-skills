# Format: meeting notes

For recorded meetings, calls, and standups. The reader wants decisions and next steps.

Template: [assets/templates/meeting-notes.md](../../assets/templates/meeting-notes.md)

## Structure

1. **Summary:** three sentences at most.
2. **Attendees:** only people named in the recording.
3. **Decisions:** what was decided, by whom, with a timestamp link.
4. **Action items:** a table of owner, task, and due date when stated.
5. **Discussion:** one short section per topic, in order.
6. **Open questions:** raised and not resolved.

## Rules

- Record only what was said. If an owner or date is not stated, write "not stated".
- Separate decisions from discussion: a decision needs a clear commitment in the recording.
- Keep personal or off-topic remarks out unless the user asks.
- Use timestamp links on decisions and action items so the reader can verify them.
