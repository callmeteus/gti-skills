# Technique packs

Distilled from Claude HT Skills (MIT). ArtDirector selects one pack from the artifact type. Do not copy the six HT skills into the project.

## infographic / data-viz

Layouts: linear-progression, binary-comparison, hub-spoke, bento-modules, funnel, dashboard-metrics, circular-flow, dense-modules.

Preserve numbers exactly. One idea per module. SVG connectors. No 3D pies.

## story (1080×1920)

Full-bleed phone screen. Fill the height. Headline 180-240px, supporting 36-48px. Stack kicker / claim / support / CTA. Keep type out of the top 210px and bottom 250px. Do not put a square feed post in the middle of the frame.

## social / carousel / announcement

One claim readable at phone-thumbnail size. Shared tokens across carousel slides. Square or 4:5, not 9:16.

## cover / typography

Type and shape are the image. Aspects: 16:9, 2.35:1, 1:1, 3:4. Rendering: flat vector, screen-print, chalk, ink.

## slides

1920x1080 artboards. One thought per slide. Shared master. Notes off the artboard.

## editorial / illustration

Modular grid. Geometric or licensed imagery only. Captions are designed.

## promotion / coupon

Offer, date, action in under a second. Do not recolor logos unless asked. No fake QR codes.
