# Visual language

This is the level Art Director aims for: professional social-media design as it appears in strong Brazilian Instagram feeds (retail promos, editorial quotes, news cards, campaign typography, holiday posts, sports, music and events, fashion collage, education). It describes how those pieces are built, so you can make new ones at the same level. It is not a set of layouts to reproduce. Read [originality.md](originality.md) before you start.

## What separates a professional piece from an HTML card

| Generic HTML card | Professional social piece |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| One flat background color | A field with texture, light, or a treated photo |
| Text centered in empty space | Type and image locked together, edge to edge |
| One font, two sizes | Two or three type voices with extreme scale contrast |
| Everything on one plane | Three to five planes of depth |
| Decoration as afterthought | Small native furniture: tags, pills, handles, marks, icons |
| Polite and symmetrical | A clear focal point, tension, and intentional cropping |

## The eight principles

1. **A hero, then type that fights for it.** Every piece has one hero: a person, an object, or a monumental word. Type does not sit next to the hero; it interlocks with it. Letters pass behind a head, a script word crosses the subject, a headline overlaps a torso.
2. **Depth in planes.** Build at least three of these, in this order: background field (color, gradient, photo, paper), back graphic (giant word, shape, crest, rays), hero (photo or cutout), front type and ornaments, finish (grain, light, vignette). See [composition.md](composition.md).
3. **Texture is always there.** Grain on everything, paper fibers, smoke, halftone, light rays, faint grids. A perfectly flat fill reads as a template. See [texture-and-ornament.md](texture-and-ornament.md).
4. **Extreme type contrast.** The headline is 6 to 12 times the size of the supporting text. Pair a massive display voice with small, widely tracked caps; add a third voice (script, brush, drip) only when it carries emotion. See [typography.md](typography.md).
5. **A tight palette with one hot accent.** One dominant field, one accent that appears in two to four places, and a neutral (white, black, cream). Photos are graded or duotoned into the palette instead of fighting it. See [photo.md](photo.md).
6. **Native furniture.** Pieces feel at home in the feed through small working elements: a pill with the category, a slanted price tag, a handle and avatar, a date, a vertical side label, a logo tab, a row of social icons, "seu logo aqui" placeholders. Use two to five, never one of each.
7. **Use the whole canvas.** Bleed photos and type off the edges, crop subjects on purpose, fill the height of stories. Empty bands above and below a centered block are the most common sign of an amateur piece.
8. **Copy in the feed's voice.** Short lines, broken for rhythm, in the user's language. A headline of two to six words, a price or date that reads in under a second, one call to action.

## Mood families

These are starting moods, described by their ingredients. Mix them; do not treat any as a fixed layout.

| Mood | Field | Type voices | Photo | Typical furniture |
|------|-------|-------------|-------|-------------------|
| Dark and dramatic | Smoke, curtain, near black with a colored glow | Drip, condensed heavy, small spaced caps | Cutouts, desaturated, rim light | Price tags, vertical lists, brand mark |
| Editorial authority | Black or off-white, generous margins | Heavy grotesk headline, bold caps quote, light caption | Side-lit portrait fading into the field | Quote mark, accent square, arrow circle, handle and avatar |
| News and information | Photo fading into a deep solid | Bold sans headline, small label pill | Full-bleed portrait with scrim | Category pill, logo tab, masthead, top bar |
| Campaign and statement | One saturated solid | Rounded heavy sans in two colors, brush number or word | None, or one cutout | Rules, a hand-drawn mark |
| Celebration and holiday | Light paper or bright gradient with rays | Echoed heavy word with gradient, script overlay, puffy 3D letters | Cutout object or symbol | Glossy 3D icons, ribbons, logo placeholder |
| Sports and energy | Aged poster paper | Brush and marker type, motion-blurred words | Big ghosted subject plus a sharp one | Blurred brush stars, crest outline |
| Music and events | Duotone photo or gradient | Wide geometric caps, letter-spaced or split words | Duotone cutout or full photo | Thin frames, orbs, X marks, dots, chevrons, diagonal bars |
| Fashion and collage | Pastel paper | Extra-condensed heavy caps, elegant script | Halftone black and white in an ornate frame | Lace, beaded frames, flowers, sparkles, tape, micro text |
| Warm education and services | Brand-colored duotone photo | Stacked syllables in heavy sans, thin italic lead | Smiling person, soft blur | Glossy 3D bubble with icon, footer with contacts |

## The reference test

Before handing over, compare each variant with that bar: would it sit next to work from a strong professional feed without looking cheaper? If it would not, the usual fixes are, in order: add a texture layer, increase type contrast, interlock type with the hero, push the hero to the edge, add two pieces of native furniture.
